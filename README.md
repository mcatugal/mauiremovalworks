# Maui Junk Quote

Create me landing page for my junk removal business named "Maui Removal Works". I'm in the process of having a logo designed so use placeholders wherever you feel a logo is necessary. The landing page needs to be simple but designed in a way that a modern contractor would professionally want their website to look like. My main goal is to generate leads by a simple process of the client texting me a photo of their junk to get a quick quote. Something like "Junk removal starting as low as $99" since my minimum price would be $99 even if it was just one item. I want the color palette to be around darker grays and black with an accent of blue. You can choose the exact shade of blue just make sure it's professional and sleek.
